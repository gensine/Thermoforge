Physics Assumptions & Debugging Insight
Assumptions: 1D steady-state conduction, uniform rod ($L=1.0\text{ m}, A=10^{-4}\text{ m}^2$), constant $k=10\text{ W/(m·K)}$, uniform source $q=10,000\text{ W/m}^3$, perfectly insulated lateral surfaces.
Boundary Conditions: Dirichlet $T(0)=100^\circ\text{C}$ and $T(L)=20^\circ\text{C}$.
Superconvergence Insight: In 1D linear FEA, nodal temperatures are algebraically exact ($E_{\text{node}} \approx 0$). Measuring error only at nodes masks truncation error. Evaluating at element midpoints reveals the true $O(h^2)$ quadratic truncation error ($E_{\text{mid}} = 125 h^2$).

Safe Hardware Integration Plan & Limitations
• Power Sequencing: Target power rails (3.3V/1.2V) must be stabilized before asserting active JTAG clock/data lines to prevent CMOS latch-up.
• Star Grounding: A common star-ground reference at host power entry isolates high-frequency digital noise between FPGA and MCU.
• Level Shifting: Bi-directional TXB0108 buffers protect the 1.2V FPGA core from 3.3V MCU digital I/O pins.
• Scope Boundaries: Physical PCB layout, wiring harnesses, firmware deployment, and FPGA bitstream synthesis are outside project scope.
